### 🪄 About Me

Hello! I'm **Nosa Peter Inwe** — a proud wanderer in both Middle-earth and the wizarding world, and in real life, an Erasmus Mundus Master's student in **Intelligent Photonics for Security, Reliability & Safety**.

Currently specializing in **Biometrics & Intelligent Vision** at the University of Eastern Finland, I spend my days (and sometimes nights) at the beautiful crossroads of optics and deep learning. Whether it's coaxing photons to reveal hidden truths or training neural networks to see like never before, I’m always chasing that perfect “aha!” moment.

- 🎓 Erasmus Mundus MSc in Intelligent Photonics  
- 🔬 4 peer-reviewed publications (IEEE & SPIE member)  
- 🏆 1st Prize – International Day of Photonics 2025  
- 🌱 Open to **internships or MSc thesis opportunities** in biomedical spectroscopy, hyperspectral imaging, or intelligent vision systems

> “You are — you have been — the bravest person I ever knew.”  
> — and yet the model still refuses to converge.
